# Inventory Rules

Inventory is deferred beyond Phase 0. The authoritative balance will derive from immutable stock-ledger entries, not an editable quantity field. Every movement will be atomic, tenant-scoped, sourced, costed, auditable, and linked to accounting when applicable. Negative stock will be blocked by default and allowed only through a permissioned configuration exception.

Current status: product and service catalogue master data is implemented. No stock ledger, stock balance, valuation, or operational inventory logic has been implemented.
