# Module Roadmap

Phase 0: documentation, secure tenancy foundation, local development, quality gates and application shell.

Phase 1: authentication, organisations, company hierarchy, memberships, permissions, audit, dashboard shell, control-centre and onboarding foundations. The bootstrap and organisation-provisioning slice is complete; hierarchy management, membership administration, audit access, subscriptions, and onboarding remain.

Phase 2: master data. Phase 3: accounting. Phase 4: purchasing and inventory. Phase 5: sales and POS. Phase 6: business administration. Phase 7: operational modules. Phase 8: industry specialisations after domain-expert validation and pilot customers.

Industry modules reuse the accounting, inventory, document, approval, audit, customer/supplier, and permission engines; they do not duplicate them.
