# Module Roadmap

Phase 0: documentation, secure tenancy foundation, local development, quality gates and application shell.

Phase 1: authentication, organisations, company hierarchy, memberships, permissions, audit, dashboard, control-centre and onboarding foundations. Completed, including bootstrap, provisioning, hierarchy, memberships/scopes/invitations, custom roles, audit access/export, support access, subscription limits, lifecycle, implementation stages, saved-context dashboard data, and end-to-end flows.

Phase 2: master data. Completed: localisation configuration, business parties, item catalogue, company payment references, and controlled business-party/catalogue imports. Phase 3: accounting is in progress; company chart and fiscal-calendar configuration is implemented, with the journal engine next. Phase 4: purchasing and inventory. Phase 5: sales and POS. Phase 6: business administration. Phase 7: operational modules. Phase 8: industry specialisations after domain-expert validation and pilot customers.

Industry modules reuse the accounting, inventory, document, approval, audit, customer/supplier, and permission engines; they do not duplicate them.
